Three record players, played from the groove up

Nothing here plays an audio file. The music is cut into a groove, and a simulated needle, arm and pickup read it back. Hiss, crackle and skips come from the shape of the groove, as they do on a real record.

Pick a machine at the top: Edison’s tinfoil phonograph of 1877, a horn gramophone of the 1920s, or a direct-drive turntable of 1979. Then work it with your hands: crank it, drop the needle, change the record, set the speed. Hover a part to see what it does.

Sound engine and records from rauchg/vinyl (MIT). Rendered with 67js. Models, all CC BY 4.0 on Sketchfab: Technics SL-1200 MK2 by igorharmendani, Patefon Gramophone by wojciechmiedziocha, Phonograph by arloopa.
